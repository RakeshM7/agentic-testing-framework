## Goal
Enter and discard edit mode on a curated dashboard.
## Preconditions
Authenticated session (Rakesh M, owner role appears).
## Steps
Click Edit: header buttons replaced by Discard; filter icon opens FILTERS panel (PAGE FILTERS and REPORT FILTERS each with Basic filters '+ filter' and Date Range '+ date range', Apply). Discard clicked. No widget add/edit controls appeared for curated dashboards.
## Outcome
See steps.
## Variations and errors
Page 'Request demo' button (top bar) opens a Freshchat conversation and auto-creates a support ticket; avoid. Chat widget can intercept clicks until page reload.
